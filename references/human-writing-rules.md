# Human Writing Rules

Use this file after the insight memo draft is complete.

The goal is not to "fake humanity."
The goal is to stop the memo from sounding like a polished AI explanation instead of a real analyst's note.

This file combines three useful lenses (used as ideas, not copied text — see [SOURCES.md](../SOURCES.md) for attribution):

- `renwei-writing`: do not edit away the person behind the judgment
- `humanizer`: remove recognizable AI sentence habits
- `dbs-ai-check`: diagnose where the prose feels too smooth, too complete, or too eager to sound deep

## Core principle

The final memo should read like:

- a smart person thought hard about the material
- kept only the judgments that matter
- wrote them down for internal use

It should not read like:

- a model trying to prove it understands everything
- a polished keynote draft
- a consulting brochure
- a motivational article about trends
- a repeated warning system that says the same thing five times

## Writing posture

### 1. Do less

Do not improve every sentence.
If a sentence is already clear and grounded, leave it alone.

### 2. Keep the person in the text

Do not sand off every rough edge.
A little asymmetry, restraint, or unresolved tension is often more human than perfect balance.

### 3. Plain words beat inflated words

Prefer:

- `是` over `意味着一种更深层的`
- `在变` over `正在重塑格局`
- `问题在于` over `本质上折射出`

If a simpler sentence says the same thing, use the simpler one.

## Strong-signal AI patterns to remove

### 1. Significance inflation

Watch for:

- 标志着
- 见证了
- 彰显了
- 折射出
- 更大图景
- 时代性变化
- 重新定义

Rule:
ordinary facts do not need to be promoted into historical turning points.

### 2. Fake depth templates

Watch for:

- `不是 X，而是 Y`
- `与其说……不如说……`
- `重要的不是……而是……`
- `本质上`
- `归根结底`

Rule:
use this kind of turn sparingly.
If it appears several times in one memo, cut most of them.

### 3. Over-even rhythm

Watch for:

- three parallel bullets with the same length
- every paragraph ending in a neat concluding line
- every insight written as a polished slogan

Rule:
one strong line is enough.
Do not make every paragraph sound quotable.

### 4. Consultancy-demo language

Watch for:

- 赋能
- 打造
- 升维
- 路径图
- 抓手
- 闭环
- 生态位
- 重塑

Rule:
use these only when they truly name something concrete.
Most of the time a simpler operational phrase is better.

### 5. Vague authority

Watch for:

- 有专家认为
- 行业普遍认为
- 市场上普遍在说
- 有观点指出

Rule:
either name the source or remove the claim.

### 6. AI-clean but lifeless prose

Sometimes the text has no obvious AI fingerprints, but still sounds synthetic because:

- every sentence is the same size
- every judgment sounds final
- nothing feels costly or observed
- all roughness has been polished out

Rule:
put back some weight.
Let a sentence stay plain if plain is what makes it believable.

### 7. Repeated warning noise

Watch for:

- several paragraphs repeating the same risk in slightly different words
- repeated "this is important / serious / cannot ignore" messaging without new mechanism
- every section trying to keep the urgency high

Rule:
if five warning lines reduce to one real judgment, keep the judgment and cut the echo.

## What to preserve

Keep these when they are real:

- a concrete observation
- a mildly awkward but honest phrase
- a sentence that sounds like how the analyst actually thinks
- a tension the memo cannot fully close
- one or two expressions with handprint, not ten

Do not flatten everything into standard "good writing."

## Final self-check

Before finishing, scan only the sentences you wrote or heavily rewrote.

Ask:

1. Did I over-explain something the reader could already infer?
2. Did I turn a normal fact into a grand historical signal?
3. Did I use `不是 X，而是 Y` or similar flips too many times?
4. Did I make too many paragraphs sound like closing lines?
5. Did I replace a plain human sentence with a prettier but less believable one?
6. If I read this aloud, does it sound like a person making a judgment, or a model performing clarity?

If unsure, make the sentence plainer, shorter, and less eager to impress.
