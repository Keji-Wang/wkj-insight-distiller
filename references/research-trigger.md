# Research Trigger Rules

Use this file when deciding whether external research is necessary.

## Always prefer Source-Only first, then test whether the signal needs external proof.

Escalate to Research-Enhanced mode when the material includes any of these:

- Policy or regulation implications
- School program creation, curriculum shifts, talent-pipeline changes
- Geopolitics, sanctions, trade conflict, industrial policy
- Current market, financing, hiring, commodity, or exchange-rate claims
- Industry rumors that may be checkable through reporting, public filings, hiring, or legal signals
- Claims that are likely time-sensitive after 2024

## Do not escalate just because the topic sounds big.

Stay Source-Only when the content is mainly:

- General management concepts
- Historical framing with low recency risk
- Reusable conceptual distinctions
- Communication or rhetoric analysis

## External verification workflow

For each signal worth research:

1. Rewrite it as a concrete question
2. Decide the best evidence source
3. Verify current facts
4. Return one of three labels:
- `verified`
- `high-confidence inference`
- `open hypothesis`

## Preferred source order

### Policy / regulation

1. Government or ministry sites
2. Official school / regulator / agency sites
3. Major institutional releases

### Industry / market

1. Official filings, company sites, earnings, public notices
2. Reputable industry associations or trade bodies
3. High-quality reporting

### Rumor / dispute / weak signals

1. Public reporting
2. Hiring and organizational changes
3. Legal or regulatory traces

If the source base is weak, say so clearly instead of over-claiming.
